## 6. New observations

1. **Unit slot ids are reused across sessions.** The same extended-uid bytes (`81 d0 11`, `81 e8 12`) appear in both replays at different map positions. They look like mission unit slots, not fixed objects (EXPERIMENTAL).
2. **Possible victim-id mapping.** Both kills fit `victim_id = (ext_uid >> 10) + 2031`, but that's only two points (EXPERIMENTAL; FORMAT_FINDINGS open question 11).
3. **Same airfield and parking spot.** The flight starts at the identical parked position (to 1 cm), consistent with a fixed spawn point.
