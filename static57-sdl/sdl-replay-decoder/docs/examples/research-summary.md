## 7. Research opportunities and recommended experiments

- **Controlled LAR shots**: Fly a test mission firing one missile type at a non-maneuvering target across a grid of launch range, altitude and aspect, including deliberate misses. Combat data here is success-biased; a grid gives the envelope edge.
- **Synchronized 8111 recording**: One short flight with 8111 capture and MARK events, following docs/TELEMETRY_8111.md. This fixes map north for X/Z and gives an independent instrument check.
- **Level acceleration and sustained-turn runs**: For each aircraft of interest: full-power level acceleration at fixed altitude and a sustained turn at fixed speed. SDL measures these directly, but combat sorties do not separate them.
- **Decode the weapon type**: 5534 of 5817 tracked weapons have no name. Decoding ECS rocket components would name them and remove the success bias.
- **TEST WPN cross-check**: 159 author releases are attributed. Compare their launch conditions with Strike Master TEST WPN predictions before any LAR change.
- **Investigate speed above 500 m/s**: The aircraft speed field saturates at 500 m/s. For fast flight use position differences, or find another speed source.
