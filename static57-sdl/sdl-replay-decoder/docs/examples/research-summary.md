## 10. Research opportunities and recommended experiments

- **Independent 8111 validation**: One real capture now confirms flight state on two aircraft. Record the short flight in docs/RECORD_ONE_FLIGHT.md to exercise flaps, airbrake and engine fields, and repeat with a feet-altimeter aircraft.
- **Flight-control and engine decoding**: telemetry-validate scores every control, engine and cockpit field against 8111. Unassigned nibbles need a flight that uses airbrake and full flaps. See docs/FINDINGS_M7_FLIGHT_SYSTEMS.md.
- **Controlled LAR shots**: Fly a test mission firing one missile type at a non-maneuvering target across a grid of launch range, altitude and aspect. This records launch conditions only: SDL has no validated miss classification, so it is not a LAR calibration.
- **Level acceleration and sustained-turn runs**: For each aircraft of interest: full-power level acceleration at fixed altitude and a sustained turn at fixed speed. SDL measures these directly, but combat sorties do not separate them.
- **Decode the weapon type**: 2142 of 6480 tracked weapons have no name because their type id has no kill in that replay. Decoding ECS rocket components would name them directly.
- **TEST WPN cross-check**: 200 author releases are attributed. Compare their launch conditions with Strike Master TEST WPN predictions before any LAR change.
- **Investigate speed above 500 m/s**: The aircraft speed field saturates at 500 m/s. For fast flight use position differences, or find another speed source.
